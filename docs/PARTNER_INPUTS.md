# Founding supplier / partner input checklist

Do not request or store sensitive details in this public repository; use a private approved channel for actual agreements, identity records, wallet addresses and account credentials.

## Before technical week one
- [ ] Confirm funding authorization and who owns/bills hosting accounts.
- [ ] Agree to initial feature scope, six-week **technical** target and weekly signoff cadence.
- [ ] Approve working brand or propose replacement; resolve basic name/trademark conflicts.
- [ ] Choose domain after live registrar availability/renewal quote and document ownership.
- [ ] Provide catalog structure and non-sensitive sample content suitable for prototype.
- [ ] Identify authorized representatives for product details, inventory, fulfillment, support and decisions.

## Before real supplier content
- [ ] Provide approved SKU list, descriptions, photographs, prices and stock rules via private channels.
- [ ] Define shipping, cancellation, returns/refunds and customer-support responsibilities.
- [ ] Specify necessary data retention, privacy and records rules.
- [ ] Sign agreement on commission basis, reporting rights, currency/fee/FX treatment, domain rights, development scope, support, termination and disputes.

## Before payment activation
- [ ] Verify business/product eligibility and merchant approval for the actual inventory and markets.
- [ ] Approve wallet-control/authorization architecture and payout/refund funding rules.
- [ ] Complete payment mechanism test results, accounting review, backups and incident runbooks.
- [ ] Complete any required financial-services review of forwarding/custody.
- [ ] Receive explicit partner/operator go-live signoff.

## Pending outside decisions
Final domain, product catalog, account ownership, provider eligibility, legal review, final contract and launch activation are all **pending**, not implicitly approved.
